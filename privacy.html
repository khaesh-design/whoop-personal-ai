Privacy Policy - WHOOP Personal AI

Last updated: November 12, 2026

WHOOP Personal AI is a personal application designed to help its authorized user review and analyze WHOOP fitness and wellness data.

Data Access

With user authorization, the application may access sleep, recovery, heart rate, heart rate variability, physiological cycles, workouts, and profile information through the WHOOP API.

Data Use

Data is used to provide personal wellness insights, activity summaries, and trend analysis. Data may be processed by a connected AI service when the user requests analysis.

Storage and Security

Access tokens and retrieved data may be stored in the application's configured hosting environment. Access is restricted to the authorized user and services required to operate the application.

Data Sharing

Data is not sold. Data may be shared with service providers necessary to deliver requested functionality.

Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only as necessary for the application's operation. The user may request deletion of stored data and revocation of access.

Contact

For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, contact the application operator using the contact email provided in the WHOOP Developer Portal.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's data processing practices change.
